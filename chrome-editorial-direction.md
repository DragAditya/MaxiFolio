# Chrome Editorial Portfolio Direction

## Core Concept

This redesign will turn the portfolio into a **digital chrome studio**: deep black space, silver reflections, cold cobalt energy, translucent glass surfaces, and carefully placed neon refractions. It is not a copy of the supplied Pinterest images. Their concepts become original web-native elements.

## Material Language

The hero becomes a sculptural software artifact floating in a black field. Chrome-framed typography, sharp silver numerals, deep-blue spatial planes, and iridescent edge light replace the current paper collage as the main language. Glass is used as a physical material with refraction and highlight, not merely a low-opacity background.

## Narrative Structure

The sequence moves from a dark “signal” intro into proof: hero artifact, curated build cards, pinned GitHub systems, toolbox, history, and a high-contrast contact frame. ASCII becomes ambient data texture. Pixel fragments appear only as secondary proof indicators.

## Interaction Rules

The main artifact expands with scroll and tilts noticeably on pointer movement. Buttons act like polished controls: they reflect light, shift in depth, and show a small chromatic edge. Project cards open like glass objects under a spotlight. Motion reduces cleanly on mobile and for reduced-motion preferences.
