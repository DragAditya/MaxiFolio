# Maximalist Skills Playground

The skills chapter will become an interactive **capability playground** rather than a static stack of four cards. Its center is a rotating “tool galaxy” with orbiting stack chips, while the existing cards become active specimens with depth, visible hover states, and a small status strip.

The scene will layer a moving skills ticker, a draggable-looking orbital control, live capability markers, and a compact “currently building” signal. Motion will remain transform- and opacity-based, pause or simplify on touch/reduced-motion contexts, and keep all factual skill content readable without requiring interaction.
