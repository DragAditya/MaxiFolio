# Contact Finale Direction

The final scene becomes a **contact control room**: a clear primary email action, layered identity card, three factual signal badges, and a compact dark console strip. The content will be arranged as an asymmetrical bento composition rather than a loose column of contact controls.

On mobile, the CTA appears immediately after the heading, followed by a focused identity artifact and a two-column link grid with tap-friendly targets. Decorative orbit and scan layers move slowly only on fine-pointer screens; the important contact paths remain static, high-contrast, and easy to reach.
