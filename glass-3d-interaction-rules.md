# Glass and 3D Interaction Rules

The supplied references are used **only as visual inspiration**. No attached image will appear on the portfolio. Their shared qualities become original web-native rules: dark space around a single focal artifact, chrome-like edge light, abrupt blue accents, high-contrast editorial typography, and ASCII texture that behaves as atmosphere rather than body copy.

Project links and primary buttons will gain a translucent glass layer with a soft bright border, colour-shifting highlight, and small directional movement on hover. The transition will be fast and tactile, while keyboard focus stays clear and visible.

The hero will have three depth layers: a slow background grid/texture, the central art frame, and foreground glass labels. Scrolling moves these layers at different rates; desktop pointer movement adds a small 3D tilt. On touch devices and for reduced-motion users, the layout stays readable with static depth and no pointer-dependent effect.
