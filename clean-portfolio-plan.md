# Clean Developer Portfolio Plan

## Direction

This version removes the abstract visual system, large decorative graphics, dense labels, and experimental wording. It becomes a clean developer portfolio that a recruiter, founder, or engineering manager can scan quickly.

The page will have a simple light background, dark text, one blue accent, familiar navigation, and clear sections: **intro, projects, skills, experience, education, achievements, and contact**. Animation will be limited to small reveal transitions and button hover feedback.

## Plain-English Copy Rules

Headlines will use short, direct sentences. Each project will say what it is, what it does, the main tools used, and a link when available. The page will avoid terms like “operating system,” “proof board,” “evidence chamber,” and decorative index language.

## Content Structure

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Intro | Explain who Aditya is and what he builds in one clear paragraph. |
| Projects | Show ALTER, DRAGX, and PeerLock with simple, useful descriptions. |
| Skills | Group languages, tools, and platforms. |
| Experience | Show business operations experience and its practical value. |
| Education and awards | Confirm MCA, BCA, CGPAs, competitions, and certifications. |
| Contact | Offer email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and phone number. |
