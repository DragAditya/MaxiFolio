# Premium Scrapbook Preloader Direction

The preloader will open as a short **scene assembly** rather than a logo splash screen. It will use the portfolio’s warm paper base, dotted-grid texture, vivid pink/lime/blue paper fragments, dark ink rules, and a centred editorial message: `ASSEMBLING THE SCENE`.

The status line and thin progress beam will reflect initial document, image, and font readiness. The main page mounts behind the opaque overlay so its resources can load without exposing a partial layout. A minimum visible interval gives cached visits a deliberate opening; resource readiness ends the progress state; Framer Motion then fades and slides the paper overlay away while the finished homepage rises into place. Reduced-motion users receive a short static handoff.

The requested component catalogue connection was inspected and is enabled, but its tool endpoint encountered a transient TLS handshake timeout during pattern research. The implementation will therefore use the project’s existing Framer Motion conventions and established scrapbook primitives rather than copy unavailable third-party source.
