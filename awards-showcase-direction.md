# Awards Showcase Direction

The recognition scene becomes a **trophy table** rather than three repeated tiles. Each record receives a different physical role: a large winner plaque, a tilted competition ticket, and a stacked credential wallet. This makes the three facts scan quickly while giving the section a richer maximalist rhythm.

The mobile sequence will avoid oversized blank icon areas. It will lead with the award title and proof label, then reveal a compact animated seal, status chip, and numbered artifact. Desktop retains an asymmetrical table with orbiting marks and staged depth; touch and reduced-motion contexts keep the recognition facts stable and legible.
